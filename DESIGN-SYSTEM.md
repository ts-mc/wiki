# Wiki design system

Use the reading styles in [globals.css](src/app/globals.css). Choose semantic tokens such as `--color-panel`, `--color-ink`, `--color-ink-muted` and `--color-portal` for surfaces, prose, metadata and accents. Use the action/on-action pair for the search button.

Keep the article title prominent, introductory paragraphs comfortably spaced, and section labels visually subordinate. Reuse the article grid, prose styles and navigation rail. At 800px and below the rail becomes a horizontal list above the article.

Keep the search field labeled for assistive technology. Give article images descriptive text and keep code, commands and wide tool results scrollable within their own container.
