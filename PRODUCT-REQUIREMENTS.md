# Wiki requirements

Java and Bedrock players and Discord members use the wiki to find instructions for their platform. Contributors need to update one guide and its illustrations without editing the page renderer.

- Search by title, topic or tag and browse by edition or Discord category.
- Preserve shareable article URLs under `/w/<edition>/<article-slug>`. Keep guide titles, steps, media and resource links understandable when opened directly.
- Expose standalone tools at `/t/<slug>` and keep instructions and results readable on a phone.

## Acceptance check

Search for an edited guide, open its result and reload the direct URL. Verify the category, step order and referenced images against the published guide JSON.
